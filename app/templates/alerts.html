How alerts work

What counts as
an alert.

A quick look at how PrediQt decides what becomes an alert, how severity is set, and what reviewing one actually does.

01
Anything flagged as fraud
Every prediction where the model's verdict comes back FRAUD becomes an alert automatically — whether it came from a single Check Transaction or one row inside a batch upload.
02
Severity follows probability
The same HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW thresholds used everywhere else in PrediQt decide severity here too — there's no separate scoring system just for alerts.
03
Marking reviewed doesn't change the verdict
"Mark Reviewed" only tracks that you've looked at it — the underlying prediction, probability and risk level stay exactly as the model produced them.
04
Admins see the whole platform
Regular users only see alerts tied to their own transactions. Admin accounts see every flagged prediction across every user, the same scoping rule used on the Dashboard.
05
Every alert links back to its source
Each alert remembers which specialist model made the call and what transaction type triggered it — nothing here is a guess, it's a record of an actual prediction.
